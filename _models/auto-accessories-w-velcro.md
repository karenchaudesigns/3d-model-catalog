### FEATURES

Keep precious cargo from sliding and tumbling around in your trunk or car floor.

Great for transporting cakes, pizzas, and other important packages.

**TIP:** Use a color that stands out so they don't get lost in the depths of your trunk…especially helpful when you're trying to find them in the dark!

**OPTIONAL:** Print in fluorescent orange so these can be used as a quick alternative to hazard triangles during roadside emergencies.

### ASSEMBLY

You'll need Velcro (or other brand of hook & loop adhesive).

You can use pre-cut strips or cut them from a roll.

**NOTE:** Use only the hard “hook” side so it latches onto the soft “loop” fabric in your car.

Apply two strips of Velcro to each piece

#### STYLE B:

Apply Velcro to each piece

With the larger surface area, you have many options on the arrangement of Velcro.

I cut 3" (76.2mm) strips from a roll and placed them diagonally (starting from the corner).

### HOW TO USE

#### Secure small items with 2 corner pieces

*(e.g. small cake boxes, gift bags/boxes) *

#### Secure larger items with 4 corner pieces

*(e.g. pizza boxes, larger cake boxes, rectangular cake/cupcake carriers, food trays)*

(For Style B) Two rack options are available – use a rack for medium and larger items to keep the item lifted off the floor (for supportive/protective/sanitary reasons).

**NOTE:** The racks are designed with rounded edges (i.e. fillets) on the top only. This is to subtly differentiate the top from the bottom (i.e. clean side vs dirty floor side). You may choose to add Velcro on the bottom side of the racks if that's preferred.
