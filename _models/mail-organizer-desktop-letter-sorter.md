I designed this mail sorter to keep incoming mail organized.

Here are some ideas on how you can use each slot of this versatile organizer:

- One per mail recipient
- Coupons & catalogs
- Junk mail or read mail to be shredded
- Outgoing mail
- Mail supplies (envelopes, postage stamps, address labels, pens, cards)

####

**Dimensions**

- **Overall**

Width: 207mm (8.1")
- Depth: 153mm (6.0")
- Height: 78mm (3.1")

- **Each slot **

Width: 48mm (1.9")
- Depth: 150mm (5.9")
- Height: 75mm (3.0")
