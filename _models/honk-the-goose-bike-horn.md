Okay, *this* might be my cutest design so far! Thanks to the Bike Gear contest for the inspiration and motivation for me to design this fun TPU squeaky toy goose bike horn – enjoy!

#### Parts to print

- Body of the goose - print in TPU95A (or softer) for it to be squeezable.
- ["The Squeakquel!"](https://makerworld.com/en/models/1447898-the-squeakquel-3d-printable-squeaky-toy#profileId-1507943) from SpoolDesigns - print on a smooth plate; PET-G recommended for outdoor use
- Bill and pegs - print in PET-G for outdoor use (or try TPU for a softer and more durable print!)

Notes/ideas:

- Attaching the goose onto a bike to use as a bike horn: I added negative parts to the print profile to make holes – these can easily be adjusted/removed to fit your needs. You can also try strapping the goose down by its neck if you prefer. The cover photo shows it attached by a simple twisty tie.
- Coloring in the eyes: I printed the body all in one color and painted in the eye with a marker. The model has the eyes as separate parts, so you can print in multi-color if you can/prefer.
- Models vs altered models folders: I shared the original models and altered versions for your convenience.
