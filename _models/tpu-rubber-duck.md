### 3D print your own squeaky toy!

To celebrate the launch of "The Squeaker!", a fully 3D printable squeaker by [@SpoolDesigns](https://www.printables.com/@SpoolDesigns), I sculpted a rubber duck to turn into a squeaky TPU duck!

Learn more on my website:
[https://www.karenchaudesigns.com/blog/3d-printed-tpu-rubber-duck-squeaky-toy](https://www.karenchaudesigns.com/blog/3d-printed-tpu-rubber-duck-squeaky-toy?utm_source=printables&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=model-page&utm_content=tpu-rubber-duck-learn-more)

#### Files

- The 3MF file is configured to be compatible with "The Squeaker!" – Make sure to print this duck in TPU95A (or softer)!
- The STL file is included for your regular non-squeaky printing purposes.

#### Required model

-  “The Squeaker!” by [@SpoolDesigns](https://www.printables.com/@SpoolDesigns) (see [website](https://www.karenchaudesigns.com/blog/3d-printed-tpu-rubber-duck-squeaky-toy?utm_source=printables&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=model-page&utm_content=tpu-rubber-duck-squeaker) for up-to-date link)

#### Updates

- v4: Made minor changes to the model to make it symmetrical

 I found that scaling it down to 90% was a happy medium of still being satisfyingly squeaky and quick and easy to print.

### Recommended Print Settings

- **Filament:** Flexible TPU (I used TPU95A)
- **Infill:** 0%
- **Walls: **3
- **Seams:** I've test printed 2 with “Random” and 2 with “Back”. The ducks with random seams squeak a little better, but don't look as good as the ones with the back seams in my opinion. “Nearest” looks like a good compromise between the two I've tried. Leave a comment on which seam settings you decide on and what you think!
