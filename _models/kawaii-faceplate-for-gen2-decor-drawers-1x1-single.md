Quick swap faceplates designed for the [GEN2 Decor Drawers](https://www.printables.com/model/964551-gen2-decor-drawers-standard) by Jerrari

Learn more:[https://www.karenchaudesigns.com/blog/karen-chau-designs-x-jerrari3d-collab](https://www.karenchaudesigns.com/blog/karen-chau-designs-x-jerrari3d-collab)

### Recommended Print Settings

- 0.4mm nozzle
- 0.2mm layer height
- 15% infill (gyroid)
- Seam position: Back (rotate faceplate so seams are on the underside of the eyes)
- Print face up
