Tired of dirty cups filling up the kitchen sink and counters?

Running out of clean cups to use because people don't know they're supposed load them into the dishwasher?

**Make it clear with a sign! **

Keep this "Borrow a Cup" sign near the cups to provide instructions to keep your communal kitchen running smoothly!

**The sign is in the shape of a coffee mug, with the message:**

BORROW A CUP
WHEN FINISHED,
PLEASE LOAD
IT INTO A
DIRTY
DISHWASHER

"DIRTY" is illustrated in the style of the round dishwasher clean/dirty/running indicator signs.

I designed this for an office that had multiple kitchens that needed more instructions. I printed them on my Bambu Lab X1C.
