Get ready for Pi Day (3.14) with a 3D printed pi-themed pie!

This miniature pie with an integrated pie tin is roughly 1:12 scale and features a full pie with a large pi-symbol in the center and the value of pi approximated to the first 35 decimals indented on the crust, with spacing for every group of 5 digits for easy parsing and as aide for memorization.

Text on the crust:

"π≈3.14159 26535 89793 23846 26433 83279 50288"

Print this miniature pie in 2 colors for a double crusted pie or in 3 colors to show off a pie filling under the π symbol embellishment.

Have a happy Pi Day and enjoy eating some real pie! 😋
