This is a cute shamrock I designed as a magnet for my March Magnets project in 2024. It features a kawaii (cute) smiling face. On the back, there is an indentation to glue a magnet – make sure the magnet is at least 2mm thick to clear the border!

With only 2 filament changes, this kawaii shamrock is a quick little print for St. Patrick's Day!
