Going to RMRRF, MRRF, 3D Printopia (fka ERRF), or SMRRF?

Pack your [Layer Connections tile by 3D Print Bunny](https://than.gs/m/1041748?affiliateCode=kcd) to participate in the community artwork project!

- The 3MF file I used for printing mine with [Polymaker Starlight Neptune PLA filament](https://us.polymaker.com/products/polylite-starlight-pla?variant=41232686710841&aff=740)
- STLs for the lid and 3 base options for different lid fit: t02 (tight), t03 (regular), t04 (loose)
