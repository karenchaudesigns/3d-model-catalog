This kit is designed for making friendship bracelets using pony beads (store-bought or 3D printed).

25 compartments are available for individual beads so you can plan out your bead pattern, visualize how it will look and rearrange the beads easily before stringing the beads.

- Tray
- Beading needle
- Pony bead (standard 6mm x 9mm size)

### Recommended Print Settings

- 0.2mm layer height
- 0.4mm nozzle

- Tray
15% infill

- Beading needle
I sliced it with seams on the back (i.e. at the tip)

- Pony bead
0% infill (2 walls)
