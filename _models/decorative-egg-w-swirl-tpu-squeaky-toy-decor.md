This is a model of an egg with a decorative twist! With its 24 swirls from the top to the bottom, it stands with a stylized design ready to decorate your mantel, desk or party table!

Enjoy it printed in your choice of filament for a decorative piece or use the TPU95 print profile along with a [3D printed squeaker](https://makerworld.com/en/models/579374-the-squeaker-the-fully-3d-printable-squeaky-toy#profileId-500144) (in PLA) to create a TPU squeaky toy egg!
