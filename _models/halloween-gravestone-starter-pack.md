I designed these gravestones to be printed flat, requiring minimal color swaps and allowing easy customization.

There are two styles of bases for you to choose depending on your style and how big and sturdy you want. They slide onto the other part of the gravestone easily.

This starter pack contains some ready-to-print designs and blank templates for you to customize. Use the slicer to add in your own text, either as engraved text to have a simple single-color print, or as raised text in a different color.
