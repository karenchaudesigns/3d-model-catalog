*Fun accessory for anyone who loves unicorns! *

#### See it in action!

#### FEATURES

- a big heart
- a unicorn horn
- unicorn ears; outlines so that string, ribbon, chain, keyring, etc can be used through the holes
- a capitalized letter (A-Z); *NOTE: I opted for a sans-serif font to make it kid-friendly*
- a blank one is provided so you can personalize with your own text

#### IDEAS ON HOW TO USE

**Charm for a backpack zipper**

- I printed two at 100% scale and glued them together back-to-back. I looped string through one of the unicorn's ear holes and tied it to a zipper pull.

**Gift bag tag**

- I printed one at 200% scale and used ribbon to tie it to the handle of the gift bag.

### Recommended Print Settings

- No supports; no brim
- Nozzle size: 0.4mm
- Layer height: 0.2mm
- Infill: 15%
