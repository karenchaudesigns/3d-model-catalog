This is sized to fit US bank notes.
It fits inside the pictured cash envelope from Chase Bank that are provided for free.

For a more decorative or festive touch, use colorful envelopes!

### Recommended Print Settings

- 0.2mm layer height
- 0.4mm nozzle
- Set the nozzle temp to the lower end of what is recommended for the filament for the letters.
- Use a textured build plate for some cool effects on the inside of the card!
