- Save your brushes by hanging them bristles down to prevent water collecting in the ferrule.
- Compatible with the Gridfinity system by Zack Freedman
- Easy to use (even single-handed!)
- Integrated drip tray
- Fits many sizes of paint brushes!

I uploaded two versions. I prefer the **angled** version that is designed to print at a 45° angle to avoid the need for supports. I printed it with a brim and it was easy to remove!
