I designed this paint brush rack so that I can hang my brushes bristles side down so they can dry properly and prevent water deteriorating the glue in the ferrule.

An important aspect I considered was ease of use – I wanted a very quick and convenient way to hang the brushes as I switch between brushes or just need to set the brush down while painting. Only one hand is required to load and unload brushes.

A built-in drip tray helps collect drips (if there are any) to protect your surfaces.
