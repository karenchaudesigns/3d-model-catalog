This is a ring dish hidden inside a stack of pancakes, on a plate!

- Can fit bracelets on the plate while holding smaller jewelry like rings inside.
- Easy print - no supports!

### Recommended Print Settings

2 options for the stack of pancakes (lid) - provided in the 3mf files:

- Single color (I wonder if this will look good with dual/tri-color or rainbow gradient filaments)
- 2 colors so that the edges of the pancakes can be a lighter color

*TIP: You can modify the color painting in the slicer if you prefer to give your pancakes a different color pattern, maybe even some darker burnt spots if you like!*
