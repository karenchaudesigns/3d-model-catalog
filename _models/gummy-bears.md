### Print it as a squeaky toy:

(See [https://www.karenchaudesigns.com/blog/3d-printed-tpu-rubber-duck-squeaky-toy)](https://www.karenchaudesigns.com/blog/3d-printed-tpu-rubber-duck-squeaky-toy?utm_source=printables&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=model-page&utm_content=gummy-bears)

1. Download The Squeaker! or The Squeaker! Mini
- **Print in PLA:** Follow the instructions closely to print out the **Reed** & **Squeaker Tube** parts and carefully assemble The Squeaker! and test for sound.

2. Download Gummy Bears
- **Print in TPU95A (or softer)**: Use one of the 3MF files provided to print the gummy bear (several 3MF files available with different settings, see notes)

### Recommended Print Settings

3 walls (harder)I initially printed these with 3 walls to try to keep it airtight, but at 100% scale, it's a bit hard/rigid – feels like a stale gummy bear candy :) I might try scaling it up and keeping the 3 walls…

 2 walls (softer & riskier)I printed a bunch of them with 2 walls and increased the top shell thickness to 1.2mm to help avoid air holes (increased the bottom shell thickness for a more consistent feel too).
*They are much softer and satisfying to squish!*
I made over a dozen of these (with the 2 walls 3MF file attached here) and brought them to 3D Printopia to share.
