A clean, infinitely expandable **Hexagonal Modular Desk Organizer** engineered specifically for support-free FDM 3D printing. Each hexagonal module locks securely to its neighbors using concealed snap-fit dovetail keys, allowing you to mix tall pen cups, smartphone docks, and shallow paperclip trays in any colorway.

- **Material:** Matte PLA or PETG (pictured in Matte Terracotta & Warm Ivory PLA)
- **Layer Height:** `0.20 mm` standard (`0.16 mm` for ultra-clean outer walls)
- **Walls / Perimeters:** 3 wall loops
- **Infill:** 15% Gyroid
- **Supports:** None required — 100% print-in-place / support-free geometry
- **Brim:** Not required on textured PEI build plates

### Included Files & Modules

1. **Tall Faceted Cup (`110 mm`)** — Ideal for pens, markers, hobby knives, and rulers.
2. **Angled Phone Cradle (`85 mm`)** — Fits standard and Pro Max smartphones with a bottom cable pass-through slot.
3. **Shallow Hardware Tray (`40 mm`)** — Great for binder clips, SD cards, nozzle kits, and washi tape.
4. **Flush Dovetail Joiner Keys** — Print at 100% infill for a rigid mechanical lock between modules.
