If you're like me and buy batteries in bulk but hate to wrap loose batteries to attach to gifts that require batteries, print this to supply batteries for your gifts in style!

This box comes with an insert to neatly organize up to 8 AA batteries. Add ribbon through the loop on the bottom of the box and thread both ends of the ribbon through the center hole of the insert and then through the slot in the lid. Wrap both ends of the ribbon around the box as pictured (or however you like).
