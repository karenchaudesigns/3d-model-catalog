Some bead kits I purchased came with short beading needles which are helpful for smaller beads, but they weren't the best for making beaded jewelry with my Friendship Bracelet Making Tools, so I designed this!

Works great with pony beads!

### Recommended Print Settings

- 0.2mm layer height
- I sliced it with seams on the back (i.e. at the tip of the Beedle)
