Print this sidewalk chalk holder to keep sticks of chalk organized and easily accessible!

The original box the chalk came in didn't have inserts and was too tightly packed to fit inserts, so I designed a box with inserts.

### Features:

- Holds 24 sticks of sidewalk chalk
- Easy to put away chalk into the 24 individual compartments keeping the chalk organized and accessible so you don't have to dig to find the next piece of chalk you want to use
- Customizable configuration – inserts are separate pieces so you can reconfigure as needed (and repurpose) – see below for ideas
- Quick and easy print – no supports needed

Ideas:

- Use fewer inserts (or no inserts) for larger compartments – great for chalk fragments or reusing after you're done using this for chalk
- Customize the inserts to be taller or shorter – just scale them accordingly to how you like it!
- Print the inserts in different colors for a super colorful and unique organizer!
