Are your markers and pens cluttering your workspace?
Print these organizers to store them beautifully while having convenient access to them!

###

### **Features:**

- **Flexible configurations **– arrange upright or lay flat to fit your space and needs

 ***NOTE: ****Horizontal storage may be the best long-term storage orientation to preserve ink (depending on pen/marker type).*

- **Modular & space-saving **- arrange to fit your space; stack standard and mini organizers
- **Expandable system** – print more as your marker/pen collection grows!

### Included:

- **“Standard” size fits most pens and markers**

 7 configurations:

- **Additional models:**

 “Mini”: same thickness as standard size but shorter – **great for travel-size pens**
- “Thick”: same height as standard size but wider compartments – **great for dry erase markers**
