I thought it would be a fun experiment that fit into the “Fall into Knitting” contest theme to make 3D printable “yarn” that you can knit!

#### STL/3MF files
- **Shorter sample lengths:** 1mm, 2mm, and 5mm
- **Longer lengths:** 4000+mm: 1mm and 2mm

#### Knitting tests
**⚠ Disclaimer:** It's been a while since I've knitted (and I wasn't that great at it).
*I'm not sure if I knit correctly, but these knitted TPU swatches twist just like my knitted scarves did 😅*

Enjoy!

### Recommended Print Settings

- 2 walls
- 0% infill

I printed with HS TPU95A and TPU95A. The high speed TPU is a bit more rigid but resulted in cleaner prints. YMMV – reminder to dry your TPU before printing!
