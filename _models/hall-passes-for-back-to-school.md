Get the classroom refreshed with new pencil-shaped hall passes!

I've designed these hall passes to fit a variety of needs (e.g. carrying styles, destinations) and you can customize them further with your color choices and adding text to make them perfect for your classroom!

#### Model variations:

- Basic
- With a slot for a lanyard/ribbon/clip
- With a hole for a keychain/string lanyard or to hang on a hook

There are blank versions so you can add your own text for your specific needs, but I've created ones with these popular labels that are ready to use:

- Bathroom
- Nurse
- Office
- Library

Keep the hall passes accessible and organized by storing them in one or more "pencil holders" -- each one has 2 compartments designed to show the labels on the erasers. Each compartment in the hall pass pencil holder is spacious enough to fit a hall pass with an attached lanyard or other small accessory. The holders work well as standalone organizers on your desk, like a traditional pencil holder! Consider mounting the hall pass holders to the wall or the side of your desk with magnets or adhesive.

#### Style ideas:

- Add the classroom number (e.g. "Rm 123") and teacher's name (e.g. "Ms. Smith") to blank areas of the hall passes
- Classic pencil style: I printed it with 4 colors
- Tri-color: Keeping the eraser and body of the pencil the same color -- each pencil in different colors to tell them apart more easily and for a fun and colorful collection!
- Achieve a two-tone style with any printer using the "multi-color" files (using only one color per layer)
- See photos for more ideas on how to use these models!
