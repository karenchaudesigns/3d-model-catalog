Inspiration:
I designed these with my own branding to bring to RMRRF 2024 to trade with other makers with their MakerChips.

I designed these poker chips with an indentation on one side to fit a 1" sticker so I could make stickers with my QR code instead of risking 3D printing them directly on the chips -- I was afraid of print issues that could cause the QR codes to be unreadable!

I made my labels by printing on sticker paper and using a Cricut to cut out the 1" circles, but you can print directly on 1" labels.
